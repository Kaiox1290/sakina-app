# Sakina privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Sakina runs on your device and does not collect your personal data.

- **Stored on your device only:** tasks, habits, progress, settings and notes. No account, server, ads, analytics or tracking.
- **Location:** approximate location is used once, when you ask, to calculate prayer times and Qibla. It stays on your device.
- **Internet:** used only to stream Quran recitation from everyayah.com and, if you turn it on with your own key, for the Rafiq assistant (your message text goes to the provider you chose). The key is stored encrypted on your device.
- **Notifications and alarms:** only for prayer, adhkar, task and focus-timer reminders.
- **Backups:** a file you save yourself, or automatic backups to a folder you choose (for example a Google Drive folder). Sakina does not upload anything on its own and does no automatic cloud transfer. Location coordinates are excluded from backups unless you turn that on, and the Rafiq key is never included. Android system cloud backup is disabled for this app.
- **Deleting data:** Settings > Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.
- **Religious content:** comes from stated sources listed in the About screen. The app gives no fatwas.
