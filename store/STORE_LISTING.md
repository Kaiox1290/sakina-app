## Full description (English)
Sakina helps you organize your life, draw closer to your Lord, and build your future, all in one calm app that works offline.

Worship
- Accurate prayer times with 10 calculation methods, Shafi/Hanafi Asr, Qibla compass and Hijri calendar
- Optional adhan sound and reminders before each prayer
- Adhkar from Hisn al-Muslim (132 chapters), morning, evening and sleep tracking
- Forty Hadith of Imam an-Nawawi with sources
- Full Quran (Uthmani text) with Al-Muyassar tafsir and recitation by Mishary Alafasy
- Khatma planner, daily wird, bookmarks and last-position memory
- Tasbih counter, Ramadan countdown

Life and growth
- Tasks, calendar and a smart schedule assistant that plans your day around prayer times
- Study planner, homework, exams, focus timer that keeps running when the app is closed
- Learn coding: goals, lessons and projects
- Habits and sport tracking
- Achievements, badges and levels with no guilt: points never go down
- "Do now / avoid now" suggestions based on your day
- Home screen widget with the next prayer and countdown

Privacy first
- Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
- Optional automatic backup to a folder you choose (including Google Drive).
- Religious content only from stated trusted sources. The app gives no fatwas.
